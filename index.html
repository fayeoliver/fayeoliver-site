About

Faye Oliver is an Irish designer based in New York. Working across disciplines, she uses form, typography, and color to build brands and tell stories. Her practice is rooted in typography and systematic design, often exploring modular systems, bold visual identities, and motion. Currently Senior Designer at Athletics.
